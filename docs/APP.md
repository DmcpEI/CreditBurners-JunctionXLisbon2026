# App — ShelfCue (React + Spring)

How the staff app is built and why. Interfaces: `docs/CONTRACT.md`; running, demo and hosting: `docs/DEMO.md`.

**Goal and working state:** a clean mobile store assistant using saved camera predictions, plus historical study. Java Spring serves React and read-only assets. Default export: `results/qwen3.8-27b-lora2-yolopad-emptycheck-alwayscompliant-full-v1/snapshots.json`; images/crops: `data/prepared-v1/assets`. 181 frames, 14 cameras, 152 areas, 1,962 observations; export totals 200 empty / 639 half / 1,123 full, including 8 fallbacks. These are model predictions, not measured inventory. Latest ML metrics and limitations belong in RESULTS, not UI copy.

**Implemented flow (Store view = simulated live view):** every camera's latest image and its shelves, labelled honestly as "Simulated live · recorded data" (header) with a pulsing red dot and "Live · last seen HH:MM" on the hero photo and camera cards (Europe/Lisbon), preserving the requested live-camera simulation; shelf cards and alerts say "Seen HH:MM". Camera cards carry "N urgent / N restock soon" badges and are sorted by urgency. Tapping a camera (card or hero photo) adds `?camera=<id>`: the left list then shows only that camera's shelves and alerts, with a "Show all cameras" button; the bell always lists alerts from every camera (88 with the LoRA export). Product cards/alerts open the selected crop. Red/amber/green backgrounds and map polygons mean empty/half/full; fallback areas stay neutral. Labels: "Restock urgently" (empty), "Restock" (half), "No restock needed". Back restores list position; URL stores selection; no inference on clicks. A tasks-first redesign was tried on 11 Oct and rejected by the user (layout kept); a Lovable brief exists only locally.

**Shelf study (rewritten 11 Oct):** loads the whole recorded day and studies it at three scopes: Whole store, one Camera (zone), one Product (English category, e.g. "Apple", across all its shelves/SKUs). Each scope: share of checks empty or half; median observation gap from empty to stocked (empty spells starting at a shelf's first image are overnight state and excluded; still-empty spells counted); lasting stock changes (a change counts unless the next image goes straight back, A-B-A flicker); "When shelves run low" stock-by-hour chart in Lisbon time, without a misleading best/worst-hour summary; ranked lists one level down (store: products most often low, products rarely low, zones with most movement; camera/product: shelves most often low) and "Longest observed empty periods", all opening the shelf evidence. Camera scope keeps image browsing and "Play the day". "Explore shelves" and the stats row are not shown on this page. One recorded day only (27 Jun 2025, ~hourly); availability and replenishment, not sales.

**Files to touch:** `SnapshotBrowser.jsx` coordinates views, navigation and the camera filter; `useSnapshots.js` handles latest-per-camera, the full-day study load, cache and URL; `ShelfDetail.jsx` evidence/focus/scroll; `CameraMap.jsx` polygons and mobile shelf buttons; `CameraPreview.jsx` desktop hero; `StoreCameras.jsx` camera cards with urgency badges; `LiveTime.jsx` the live dot + last-seen label; `AlertsPanel.jsx` and `TaskList.jsx` staff tasks; `insights.js` (tested in `ui/tests/insights.test.js`) all study maths; `ShelfStudy.jsx` renders the study. All under `ui/src/features/snapshots/`. `ui/src/style.css` holds responsive styling; later rules override earlier iterations. `api/src/main/java/team/creditburners/` uses controller/service/repository/dto; `SnapshotRepository.java` selects the saved export at startup. Contract and source data are not edited by the browser.

**Product assessment:** latest snapshots supply `product_check: not_assessed`; prefer this field to legacy run-name inference in `assessmentMetadata.js`. Fixed compliant=true is not a verified product match. User explicitly removed product-not-checked messages from the staff UI; preserve semantic gating so no false match badge appears. Do not reintroduce technical notices just to fill space.

**Design decisions and rejected attempts:** the app is named **ShelfCue**; Sensei remains the challenge name. The supplied transparent artwork is preserved in `ui/public/brand/shelfcue-logo.png`, framed by `BrandSymbol.jsx`; `shelfcue-mark.svg` is the matching small favicon. User prefers clean photo-led UI, neutral borders with colored backgrounds (not colored card borders), compact mobile controls. No colored name badge. Startup splash/cart animation and intro toast were rejected and removed. The hero title renders immediately in `TypedHeadline.jsx`; the typing animation was removed after review. Do not recreate intro overlays. Desktop preview is borderless/transparent and hidden on mobile. Study metrics stack below 380px to keep values and explanations readable.

**Run, verify and stop:** commands and fresh-validation handoff are in DEMO. Node/Java17+/Maven and locally prepared images are required. Builds, ML smoke tests and 12 UI tests pass (11 Oct); live isolated-port API verified all 181 frames/assets, browser checked 14 cameras/152 latest shelves, no mobile overflow and study-to-crop navigation. Test servers were stopped. Physical phone, public hosting/QR and full camera-geometry sweep were not verified. Do not stop a user-owned IntelliJ process; stop only exact processes you started.
